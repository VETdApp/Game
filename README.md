# Medieval Battle Simulator

This is a small browser-based game. Two teams of 10 soldiers (green vs yellow) battle
inside an enclosed arena. Soldiers are circles with coloured outlines and swords.
They move, rotate and swing their swords randomly. If the sharp end of a sword
hits an enemy, that soldier dies and becomes a dark grey obstacle. When one team
runs out of soldiers the game ends.

Open `index.html` in your browser to play.
